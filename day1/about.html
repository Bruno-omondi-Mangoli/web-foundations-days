About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for jotting down short reminders, ideas and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps everything in one place so you can find and review your notes whenever you need them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your note into the text box.
  3. Click "Add Note" or press Enter.
  4. Read your notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a note in one step
  • See all your notes in a single list
  • Works on phones and computers
  • Simple, distraction-free design

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
